**When two classes fit, the first wins**

1. not a cocoon → **7 Exclude**
2. parasitoid larvae → **1 Parasites** (even if damaged)
3. wall broken / torn / crushed:
   prepupa intact → **9**, otherwise → **3**
4. chalkbrood → **4**
5. prepupa and pollen → **8**
6. otherwise: **0**, **5** or **2**

**Classes**

- **0** one live prepupa, cell intact
- **2** empty, wall intact (dented is still intact)
- **5** pollen, no prepupa
- **6** can't decide → goes to review, not a final label
